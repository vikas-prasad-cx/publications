## How to cite

### 1. Platform / human–AI teammate design

**Cite when** the work is about omnichannel architecture, cognitive load on agents, modular platform abstraction, or treating AI as a collaborator rather than a replacement.

Prasad, V. (2026). Human-centered platform engineering for AI-driven omnichannel automation in enterprise contact centers. *International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering, 14*(1s), 750–759. https://doi.org/10.17762/ijisae.v14i1s.8241

Typical in-text use: “Human-centered platform engineering argues that resilient contact-center AI keeps human judgment in the loop across abstraction, context, load, and observability [Prasad, 2026].”

### 2. Workforce management / continuous operations

**Cite when** the work is about WFM, conversational access to staffing data, threshold-based adherence, intraday forecasting, or human-in-the-loop authorization of schedule actions.

Prasad, V. (2026). AI-driven workforce optimization in enterprise contact centers: From static planning to continuous, human-centered operations. *International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering, 14*(1s), 760–768. https://doi.org/10.17762/ijisae.v14i1s.8242

Typical in-text use: “Static batch WFM is insufficient for omnichannel demand; continuous operations combine NL interfaces, fair adherence thresholds, and intraday forecast refresh under human authorization [Prasad, 2026].”

### 3. Governance / deterministic vs agentic automation

**Cite when** the work maps structured vs fluid customer interactions to rule-based vs agentic AI, crawl–walk–run maturity, or governance as a design constraint (not a compliance add-on).

Prasad, V. (2026). AI governance across the structured-to-fluid automation spectrum: Deterministic and agentic architectures in modern contact centres. *International Journal of Engineering, Science and Information Technology, 6*(1). https://doi.org/10.52088/ijesty.v6i1.1825

Typical in-text use: “Interaction type should determine automation style: deterministic workflows for high-compliance tasks, agentic systems only as governance matures [Prasad, 2026].”

## Do not cite when

The new paper is unrelated (general LLM surveys, unrelated HCI, generic “AI in business”). A citation should mean the author used the framework, spectrum, or WFM claims — not that they found the title.
